How to play

Goal: uncover every square that isn't a mine. You never have to uncover the mines themselves — flagging them is optional.

Numbers tell you how many mines touch that square, counting all eight neighbours including diagonals. A blank square touches none, so its neighbours open automatically — one click can clear a whole region.

Left click uncovers. Right click flags a square you think is a mine; flagged squares can't be uncovered by accident. Middle click (or both buttons) on a number whose flags already add up uncovers its remaining neighbours — fast, but it trusts your flags.

Keyboard: ↑↓←→ to move, Enter to uncover, F to flag, C to chord.

Your first click is always safe — the mines are laid out after it, never underneath it.
